# Snake Blitz Phase 4 test plan

1. Launch online room, join from second browser, verify role split and role swaps.
2. Launch Local Co-op, enter two names, verify WASD + mouse work simultaneously and displayed names swap roles.
3. Drive at least 800 px from spawn; verify camera remains calm inside dead-zone then scrolls with the tank.
4. Verify rocks, wrecks, towers and bases block the tank.
5. Check the circular tactical minimap: tank heading/location, world boundary, roads/structures and camera footprint should update while driving; active bosses should appear as a pulsing red marker.
6. Confirm payout HUD starts ~10×, approaches 5× at 10s, 3× at 20s, 1× at 30s, and 0× at 90s.
7. Identify and fight normal, bomber, venom and cash snakes. Verify venom projectiles damage tank and cash snakes drop crates.
8. Clear a wave and inspect seven armory upgrades; verify each progress bar advances.
9. Buy Armour/Engine/Autoloader/Scavenger/Ordnance over a test run and verify visible tank additions.
10. Increase Ordnance to Lv3+ and Lv5+ to verify heavy-shell/rocket presentation and splash explosions.
11. Reach/test Wave 10; verify boss state changes, telegraph, exposed window, defeat flow and role swap.
12. Spin boss reward; confirm wheel animates, reward is added to boost HUD, survives into later wave, cycles with right-click and activates with middle-click.
13. Verify Phoenix boost creates an automatic revive and Nuke clears normal enemies but does not delete a boss.
14. Restart after game-over and verify run state/upgrades/boosts reset.

15. Start a run with Music and SFX ON; verify one of the three supplied tracks begins after gameplay starts and automatically advances to the next track when it ends.
16. Toggle Music OFF/ON from the game-over screen and lobby; verify the playlist pauses/resumes and the setting persists after a refresh.
17. Toggle SFX OFF/ON; verify cannon, impacts and engine mute/unmute independently of music.
18. At idle, listen for the low pulsing tracked-vehicle idle; accelerate, cruise and stop to verify smooth engine ramp-up/ramp-down. Buy Engine upgrades and confirm the engine note becomes higher/richer at the same driving load.
19. Fire standard shells, then heavy shells/rockets after Ordnance upgrades; verify the cannon is a low-frequency blast/mechanical crack rather than the old arcade pew.
